The lineup

Built where the work happens.

We don't design from a conference room. Three rules decide what ships.

Field first

We come from job sites, engine bays and long highway miles. If a feature wouldn't survive a real workday, it doesn't ship.

Out of sight

The best tool is the one you forget is running. Ours plan, log and track in the background and only speak up when it matters.

Straight deals

Plain prices you can read in one glance. No sales calls, no long contracts, and you can leave whenever you want.

Independent by design.

Ghost Systems LLC is an independent software company headquartered in Iowa. We design, build and run our own products end to end, from the first sketch to the servers they live on.

No outside investors and no board to impress. We answer to the people who use our software.
